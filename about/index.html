About HyMotion

Built around the way gyms actually work.

HyMotion is a gym management system focused on turning everyday gym operations into one organized, practical workflow.

Explore HyMotion Try the Demo

Why HyMotion exists

Gym operations shouldn't feel scattered.

Most gyms don't lack effort or discipline — they lack one place where the day comes together. So answers live in memory, records live in different tools, and the owner finds out last.

Notebook+ Excel+ WhatsApp+ Attendance device+ Sales records+ Staff information
So we built HyMotion

One connected operational system.

HyMotion brings the daily work of a gym — members, check-ins, sales, payments, staff — into one system the whole team works from.

Our approach

Built for the people who run the gym.

Instead of forcing gym teams to adapt to complicated software, HyMotion is designed around how the gym already works. Three principles guide every screen.

Practical
  • The system should solve real daily problems — at the desk, on the floor, at closing time.
Connected
  • Different parts of the gym operation should work together — not in separate files and chats.
Clear
  • Staff should understand what to do without unnecessary complexity or training manuals.

Today

What HyMotion is focused on today.

Today, our focus is the HyMotion Local Edition — a practical gym management system designed for real day-to-day operation.

That means a system installed inside the gym, working on the gym's own network, keeping the gym's data in a local database with backup and recovery — while the owner, reception, trainer, and HR each work from their own device with their own permissions.

Direction

Built for today. Ready for what's next.

Today, we are focused on building a strong local foundation for gyms. As HyMotion evolves, that foundation can extend into connected and cloud experiences.
