The product

Everything the gym runs on. One system.

HyMotion is organized the way a gym is organized — front desk, finance, people, catalog, and system. These are the real areas of the product, with the real screens.

Try the Demo See a full gym day

Front Desk

Where the gym day happens.

The desk that never stops: shift, members, check-in, sales, and follow-ups — one workflow, not five windows.

  • ✓Current Shift — cash float, collection, close
  • ✓Members — profiles, memberships, freeze & renew
  • ✓Member Attendance — scan, search, live feed
  • ✓Sales — memberships and products on one invoice
  • ✓Call Sheet & Access Cards

Members

Every member, one record.

Profile, membership status, payments, visits, and card — connected. Reception answers in seconds what used to need three notebooks and a phone call.

  • ✓Membership statuses — active, expiring, frozen, ended
  • ✓Outstanding balances and debtors
  • ✓Attendance history per member
  • ✓Contracts and renewal trail

Attendance

Check-in that keeps up with the door.

At 7 PM the door is the bottleneck. Scan a card, show a QR, or search a name — the member is in, the record is kept, and the floor count is live.

  • ✓Manual · barcode · QR · PVC cards
  • ✓Biometric integration
  • ✓Live method split and occupancy
See the attendance ecosystem

Team & HR

The people side of the gym, in the same system.

Employees, departments, positions, schedules, leaves, payroll — plus employee attendance with biometric support. Each staff account sees only what its role allows.

  • ✓Roles & permissions catalog
  • ✓Employee attendance — including biometric devices
  • ✓Departments, positions, schedule, leaves, payroll

Management

The owner's chair.

Plans, activities, reports, and the dashboard that answers the only question that matters at 11 PM: how did today go?

  • ✓Reports — sales, memberships, attendance, staff & shifts
  • ✓Plans & activities catalog
  • ✓Owner dashboard — today at a glance
  • ✓Backup & recovery
